Structure

Who does what.

Executives

Direction, client relationships, and scoping. They're the people you talk to when a project starts and the ones who agree the plan with you.

Board

Oversight and investment, keeping the studio steady and pointed in the right direction as it grows.

Engineers

The people writing the code. Senior engineers lead the work and support the junior engineers learning the trade beside them.

FAQ

Common questions.

Who will I actually talk to?

Our management handles scoping and keeps in touch through the work, so you always have a single point of contact rather than a crowd of people.

Do the engineers work directly with clients?

Management runs communication and scoping, and brings engineers in directly when technical detail matters. That keeps things clear without cutting you off from the people building your systems.

How is work assigned to engineers?

Tasks are matched to the engineer best suited to them, with senior engineers reviewing the work before it's handed over.

Is the team growing?

Yes. We're always looking for scripters and programmers who can prove their work, across every level of the studio.
