Payment

Pay per task.

We work on a per-task basis, meaning you pay for the specific task or feature you hire us for, not a flat retainer. Rates are scoped and agreed on before work begins, so there are no surprises.

How it works

Scoped first. Then built.

1

Scope it

Tell us what you need built. Our management scopes it out with you: the features, the edge cases, and what "done" looks like.

2

Agree the rate

We price that scope and settle the rate with you. Nothing begins until you've agreed to it.

3

We build it

We build the task or feature you paid for. When it's delivered, you pay for that work, not a monthly retainer.

FAQ

Common questions.

How is a rate decided?

The scope decides it. We look at the size of the work, how complex it is, and how much it has to integrate with, then agree the rate with you before anything starts.

What happens if the scope changes?

If the work grows beyond what we scoped, we agree the new scope and rate with you before doing any of it. You won't be charged for changes you didn't approve.

Do you charge a retainer?

No. There's no flat retainer to keep us on standby. You pay per task, for the specific work you ask us to do.

Can we set up an ongoing arrangement?

Yes. If you want steady support rather than one-off tasks, ongoing contracts are available and can be scoped the same way.

Can I get the terms in writing?

Contracts are available on request. Just ask and we'll put the scope, deliverables, and rate in writing before work begins.
