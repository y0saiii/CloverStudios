FAQ

Before you email.

What happens after I get in touch?

Our management reads your email and scopes the project out with you. If it's a good fit, the rate is agreed before any work begins.

How much detail should I include?

As much as you reasonably can. What you want built, where it's going, and how you'd like to work gives us enough to scope it properly.

What if I don't know exactly what I need yet?

That's fine. Describe the goal or the problem and we'll work out the scope with you. You don't need a finished spec to start the conversation.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. A single bug fix or one system is a perfectly good reason to get in touch. One-off tasks are scoped and priced the same way as larger builds.

Can I get a contract for the work?

Contracts are available on request. If you'd like the scope, deliverables, and rate in writing, just say so and we'll draw them up.
